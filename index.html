About

I am a PhD student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Shanghai Innovation Institution, specializing in robot learning.

My work spans dexterous manipulation, real-time visual perception, and memory for foundation models. Feel free to email me to discuss research or collaboration.

Research Interests

  • Robot learning: dexterous manipulation, reinforcement learning, language-guided robotics
  • Perception: event-driven vision, real-time tracking, 6D pose estimation
  • Memory: memory-augmented foundation models and agents
